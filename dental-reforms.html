HELPING DENTAL REFORMS BUILD A STRONGER LOCAL DIGITAL PRESENCE.

CLIENT: Dental Reforms
LOCATION: Naupada, Thane, Maharashtra
BUSINESS TYPE: Dental Clinic & Advanced Dentistry
LIVE CLINIC · NAUPADA, THANE
THE BUSINESS

Dental Reforms is an advanced dentistry and smile correction clinic located in Naupada, Thane, led by Dr. Dipika Dodeja.

THE CHALLENGE

"Build stronger local visibility while creating educational content that communicates clinical expertise and builds patient trust."

LOCAL VISIBILITY

Needed stronger Google Maps rankings and local search visibility for implant & root canal searches across Thane.

PATIENT EDUCATION

Patients experienced fear regarding root canals and dental pain that generic stock flyers couldn't address.

LOCAL TRUST

Needed authentic clinic photography and patient-facing video content to build confidence before scheduling.

WHAT WE WORKED ON
LOCAL SEO GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE INSTAGRAM MANAGEMENT EDUCATIONAL CONTENT REELS CLINICAL VIDEO PRODUCTION REVIEW WORKFLOW
EXECUTION

LOCAL VISIBILITY

Audited and restructured the Google Business Profile, aligned local citations with Naupada pin-codes, and targeted high-intent keywords like "root canal specialist Thane" and "dental implants Naupada".

CONTENT SYSTEM

Scripted, shot, and edited 12 doctor-led educational reels and 8 graphic carousels directly addressing patient anxiety around pain, veneers, and procedure timelines.

PATIENT TRUST

Produced an on-location 4K clinical video and hygiene tour with Dr. Dipika Dodeja, demonstrating sterilization standards and clinical care without stock footage.

CONVERSION EXPERIENCE

Created a 1-tap WhatsApp consultation workflow and post-consultation review prompt cards that turned satisfied patients into authentic Google reviews.
