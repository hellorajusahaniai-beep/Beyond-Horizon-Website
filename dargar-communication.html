CASE STUDY / CLIENT WORK

HELPING DARGAR COMMUNICATION BUILD A STRONGER LOCAL DIGITAL PRESENCE.

CLIENT: Dargar Communication
LOCATION: Kalyan West, Maharashtra
BUSINESS TYPE: Mobile & Electronics Retail
ACTIVE RETAIL STORE · KALYAN WEST
THE CHALLENGE

"Establish high-intent local visibility on Google Maps and create engaging storefront and product content to drive retail footfall in a competitive market."

LOCAL VISIBILITY

Store lacked high-intent local visibility on Google Maps and search for smartphone buyers in Kalyan.

PRODUCT SPEED

No consistent digital channel to showcase new smartphone unboxings, festive offers, and gadget accessories.

LOCAL TRUST

Needed verified customer reviews and authentic local branding to compete with large electronic chains.

WHAT WE WORKED ON
GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE LOCAL VISIBILITY INSTAGRAM & FACEBOOK PRODUCT CONTENT STOREFRONT CONTENT REVIEW SYSTEM
EXECUTION

LOCAL VISIBILITY

Restructured and fully claimed the Google Business Profile, added real storefront photos, configured store categories, and optimized local search rankings across Kalyan West.

STOREFRONT & PRODUCT CONTENT

Produced hands-on unboxing reels, festive offer carousels, and high-quality product photography highlighting genuine stock, EMI schemes, and warranty coverage.

RETAIL ATTENTION SYSTEM

Built a systematic content calendar across Instagram and Facebook syncing with new phone launches and regional festivals to keep the store top-of-mind.

CUSTOMER INQUIRIES & REVIEWS

Deployed counter QR cards and follow-up templates enabling happy buyers to leave 5-star Google reviews and message directly on WhatsApp for stock availability.
